## 🔬 Model Overview & Architecture
While machine learning excels at identifying patterns in high-dimensional genomic features, it lacks built-in awareness of biochemical pathway architecture or clinical guidelines. To address this, XMARs implements a **deterministic clinical rule engine** grounded in established oncological literature.

This model receives predicted somatic driver variants and their Variant Allele Frequencies (VAF) from upstream agents, queries the `DrugKnowledgeBase`, and computes precise **Drug Resistance Probability Scores** ($\mathcal{R}$) and **Drug Sensitivity Scores** ($\mathcal{S} = 1 - \mathcal{R}$) clamped between `0.05` and `0.95`.

## 🧬 Clinical Rules & Scoring Metrics

The engine categorizes genomic variants into three primary pharmacogenomic classes:

### 1. Activating/Sensitizing Mutations
*   **Biological Mechanism**: Somatic mutations occurring within the tyrosine kinase binding pocket of a receptor target increase the tumor's dependency on that pathway, making it highly sensitive to target-specific inhibitors.
*   **Logic**: If the drug targets a gene labeled as a sensitivity marker:
    $$\mathcal{R}_{\text{new}} = \mathcal{R}_{\text{base}} - (0.3 + \text{VAF} \times 0.15)$$
*   **Markers (`resistance_predictor.py`)**:
    *   `EGFR` mutation $\rightarrow$ sensitivity to EGFR TKIs (Osimertinib, Erlotinib, Gefitinib).
    *   `ALK` or `ROS1` rearrangement $\rightarrow$ sensitivity to ALK/ROS1 inhibitors (Crizotinib, Alectinib).
    *   `BRAF` mutation $\rightarrow$ sensitivity to BRAF/MEK inhibitors.

### 2. Bypass Resistance Mutations
*   **Biological Mechanism**: Downstream pathway mutations or amplification of alternative receptors bypass the drug block, restoring proliferative signals despite successful upstream inhibition.
*   **Logic**: If a bypass resistance gene is mutated, the drug's resistance score is boosted to a designated threshold:
    $$\mathcal{R} = \max(\mathcal{R}_{\text{base}}, \text{bypass\_score} \times (0.5 + \text{VAF} \times 0.5))$$
*   **Markers (`resistance_predictor.py`)**:
    *   `KRAS` mutation $\rightarrow$ resistance to EGFR TKIs (Score: **0.80**).
    *   `PIK3CA` mutation $\rightarrow$ resistance to EGFR and MEK inhibitors (Score: **0.55**).
    *   `PTEN` loss or `MET` amplification $\rightarrow$ resistance to EGFR TKIs (Scores: **0.60** and **0.65**).
    *   `RB1` loss $\rightarrow$ resistance to CDK4/6 inhibitors (Score: **0.85**).
    *   `TP53` mutation $\rightarrow$ resistance to MDM2 inhibitors (Score: **0.90**).

### 3. DNA Repair Deficiencies & Synthetic Lethality
*   **Biological Mechanism**: Inactivation of homologous recombination repair pathways prevents cells from fixing double-strand breaks, inducing cell death when exposed to DNA-damaging or repair-blocking agents.
*   **Logic**: If `BRCA1`, `BRCA2`, or `ATM` are mutated, the resistance score of PARP inhibitors decreases:
    $$\mathcal{R}_{\text{new}} = \mathcal{R}_{\text{base}} - 0.15 \times \text{number of repair mutations}$$
